## Source variants and gaps

- Q1's solution ends after the light-gathering formula. It does not explicitly
  substitute the values or identify an answer letter.
- Q3's solution restates an Earth-relative “third cosmic speed” question,
  while its derivation and 42.42 km/s final value use the Sun-relative speed
  asked for in the question paper. A visible catalog note records this.
- Q5 asks for the orbital period of the Galaxy–Andromeda pair. The supplied
  S5 file asks for the time for a 6 m telescope at wavelength
  5 × 10^-7 m to detect orbital motion. Its official reasoning includes the
  period formula, but its approximately 10^3-year final value is a detection
  time. The solution is marked `variant` and introduced by a visible note.
- Q9 asks for the Moon to have 50% of the Sun's magnitude. The supplied S9
  file instead asks for equal brightness and concludes 568.88 km. It also
  prints 348000 km initially but substitutes 384000 km. The solution is
  marked `variant` and introduced by a visible note.
- Q13's question file refers to a separately issued blind sky map and an
  IAU-code Table 1. Neither is included. The surviving annotated solution
  charts are placed with the solution, and the missing question material is
  recorded in the catalog note.

## Printed inconsistencies retained

- Q2's official solution prints 0.0098 rad and 33.7 arcmin in the same
  angular-diameter calculation. These values do not agree. Its explanation
  also says sunrise is seen later, although its preceding description is
  of an earlier apparent sunrise. The source expressions are retained and
  flagged with `% sic` comments and a visible catalog note.
- Q7's official solution has an intermediate negative expression for the
  tension of ball (c), immediately followed by the positive final tension.
  The transcription follows the final expression and flags the intermediate
  source sign error in a comment.
- Q11 calls tropical zodiac sign intervals “constellations” and attributes
  constant areal velocity to Kepler's third law. Its stated dates disagree
  with its 223-day interval, while the 2022 midpoint date and one separate
  interval are blank. These gaps remain explicit. The subsequent source
  areas and velocities are retained. Catalog notes and `% sic` comments
  identify the source problems.
- Q12's printed relative-velocity subtraction and squared-speed expression
  use the asteroid speed where the spacecraft speed would be expected.
  Its derivation and final expression are retained and introduced by a
  visible note. A duplicated, garbled phrase in the question's velocity
  description is consolidated, with an editorial comment identifying this.

## Independent review

A separate review compared all 15 statements and all 13 supplied solution
entries with the 18 original PDFs. The multiple-choice answer values,
trigonometric and orbital formulae, Q11 dates, intervals, areas and velocity
components, Q12's printed relative-velocity derivation, and all eight Q13
answers were checked against source text. Rendered pages were used to resolve
formula layout and inspect the figures. All 18 source Git blob checksums
independently match the pinned manifest.

The review made the following corrections.

- Removed the inferred 10-point allocations for Q1–Q10. Their paper states
  only a 100-point section total. Each editable statement now explains the
  shared total and the absence of individual allocations. The crop statements
  include the original section heading and answer-sheet instructions.
- Moved the Q11 statement crop starts on source pp. 3–4 upward to include
  the complete first line and the complete part D heading.
- Moved the Q10 solution crop below the preceding answer choices, removing
  clipped subscript fragments from the top edge.
- Expanded the Q9 solution diagram crop to include the complete lunar ellipse
  and the left observer label.
- Expanded the Q12 solution diagram crop to include the complete left arrow,
  asteroid endpoint and A label.

All 20 figure recipes were rendered and inspected in a contact sheet. The two
expanded solution diagrams were inspected again individually. All statement
and solution crop rectangles lie within their pages. A glyph-boundary check
found no partial text characters at the revised source crop edges.

After the corrections, both 2022-only editions rebuilt successfully. The crop
edition has 49 pages and 83 valid internal links. The editable edition has
38 pages and 103 valid internal links. Its final LaTeX log contains no
undefined or multiply defined references, missing characters, or overfull
boxes. Sampled rendered output pages confirmed the shared-score note, Q11
continuations and the cleaned Q10 solution. The two variant keys, two absent
night-observation keys and missing Q13 handouts remain explicitly identified.
