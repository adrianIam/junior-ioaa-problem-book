The Sun's angular diameter as observed from Saturn is 9.54 times smaller
than its angular diameter from Earth.
\ioaafig{2022_TH_Q06-s1.pdf}
Using $d_{\mathrm{Sun}}=r_{\mathrm{Saturn-Sun}}\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}}
=r_{\mathrm{Earth-Sun}}\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Earth}}$,
\begin{align*}
\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}}
  &=\frac1{9.54}\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Earth}}=\frac{32'}{9.54}\\
  &\approx3.35'\approx0.00097\,\mathrm{rad}\approx0.001\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{align*}
The answer is (a).
