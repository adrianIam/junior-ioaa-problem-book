Firstly calculate the geocentric orbit height using Newton's law of gravitation and $T=23$ hours 56 minutes 4 seconds.
\[\frac{GMm}{d^2}=\frac{mv^2}{d},\qquad v=\frac{2\pi d}{T}.\]
\hfill(3 points)
Solving gives $d=(GMT^2/(4\pi^2))^{1/3}$. \hfill(1 point)
Substitute the correct values to obtain $d=4.216\times10^7\,\mathrm m$. \hfill(1 point)
Construct a triangle. The law of sines gives
\[\frac{\sin(90^\circ+a)}{d}=\frac{\sin(90^\circ-(\phi+a))}{r},\]
where $r$ is Earth's radius, $a$ the altitude, and $\phi$ Kathmandu's latitude. \hfill(5 points)
Simplifying,
\[\cos a\,\frac rd=\cos\phi\cos a-\sin\phi\sin a,\]
\[1-\tan\phi\tan a=\frac r{d\cos\phi}.\]
\hfill(2 points)
Rearranging and solving,
\[a=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{1-r/(d\cos\phi)}{\tan\phi}\right).\]
\hfill(2 points)
Plugging in the values gives $a=57.64^\circ$. \hfill(1 point)
