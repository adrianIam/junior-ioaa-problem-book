The luminosity is the total radiation emitted per unit time through the
star's entire surface at all wavelengths. The Stefan--Boltzmann law gives
\[
L_\Sigma=4\pi R_\Sigma^2\sigma T_\Sigma^4,
\qquad L_S=4\pi R_S^2\sigma T_S^4,
\qquad L_\Sigma=100L_S.
\]
Hence
\[
\frac{R_\Sigma^2}{R_S^2}=10^2\frac{T_S^4}{T_\Sigma^4}=10^2\times2^4,
\qquad \frac{R_\Sigma}{R_S}=40.
\]
The answer is (a).
