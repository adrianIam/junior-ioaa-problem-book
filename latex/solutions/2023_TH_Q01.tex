The Sun covers approximately $361^\circ$ on the celestial sphere in 24 hours, thus the velocity will be
\[
v=\frac{361}{24\times3600}\ \mathrm{degrees\,s^{-1}}.
\]
\hfill\mbox{(5 points)}

The Sun will need to move $s=(32/60)/\cos(\pi/4)$ degrees to cross the horizon. Therefore, the time it will need is
\[
t=\frac{s}{v}=\frac{(32/60)/\cos(\pi/4)}{361/(24\times3600)}=3\ \mathrm{minutes}.
\]
\hfill\mbox{(5 points)}
