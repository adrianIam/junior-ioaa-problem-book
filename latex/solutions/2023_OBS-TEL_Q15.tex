\textbf{Working for part (d).} At the autumnal equinox on 23 September 2023
at about 07:47 UTC, the Sun's right ascension was \(12^{\mathrm h}\).
It moves about \(1^\circ\) east per day, or \(360^\circ/365.25\) days.
Over five days the advance is about \(4.93^\circ\), or about 19--20 minutes of
right ascension at 4 minutes per degree. The key gives approximately
\(12^{\mathrm h}19^{\mathrm m}\).
