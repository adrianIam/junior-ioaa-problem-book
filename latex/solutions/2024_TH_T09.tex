\begin{enumerate}
\item The acceleration due to gravity becomes $a=-\tfrac43G\pi\rho r$. \hfill(2 points)
This is $a=-k^2r$ with $k=\sqrt{\tfrac43G\pi\rho}$. It is the equation for a harmonic oscillator, as given in the hint. Its period is $T=2\pi/k$. \hfill(1 point)
Thus $T=\sqrt{3\pi/(G\rho)}$. \hfill(2 points)
\item From the expression for $V$, the potential difference between $r=R$ and $r=0$ is $\Delta V_{\mathrm{int}}=GM/(2R)$. \hfill(3 points)
Finally, to escape,
\[\frac12mv_{\mathrm{esc}}^2-\frac43Gm\rho\pi R^2-\Delta V_{\mathrm{int}}=0.\]
\hfill(2 points)
% sic: The official energy equation subtracts a specific potential from energies without a factor of m. The final speed has consistent units.
Collecting terms gives $v_{\mathrm{esc}}=2R\sqrt{G\pi\rho}$. \hfill(5 points)
\end{enumerate}
