The degree of collection of light from a star, $g_c$, by a telescope is
defined as the ratio of the area of the circle whose diameter is the
objective diameter and the area of the observer's pupil,
\[
g_c=\frac{\frac\pi4D_{\mathrm{objective\ telescope}}^2}
{\frac\pi4D_{\mathrm{pupil\ eye}}^2}
=\left(\frac{D_{\mathrm{objective\ telescope}}}{D_{\mathrm{pupil\ eye}}}\right)^2,
\qquad D_{\mathrm{objective\ telescope}}>D_{\mathrm{pupil\ eye}},\quad g_c>1.
\]
