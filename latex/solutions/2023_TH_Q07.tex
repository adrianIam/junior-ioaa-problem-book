The radial and transverse velocities of Barnard's star can be extracted from the given information.
For the radial velocity, use the non-relativistic Doppler formula,
\[
v_r=\frac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda}c
=\frac{656.044-656.281}{656.281}\times2.998\times10^5
=-108.27\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.
\]
\hfill\mbox{(3 points)}

For the tangential velocity, first evaluate the distance from the parallax, $d=1/0.552=1.81\,\mathrm{pc}$.
The proper motion of 10.31 arcseconds/year corresponds to
\[
v_t=\mu d=\frac{10.31}{206265}\times1.81\times3.086\times10^{13}
\times\frac{1}{365.2422\times24\times3600}
=88.55\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.
\]
\hfill\mbox{(4 points)}

The speed follows from Pythagoras' theorem,
\[
v=\sqrt{108.27^2+88.55^2}=139.87\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.
\]
\hfill\mbox{(3 points)}
