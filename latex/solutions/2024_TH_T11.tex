\begin{enumerate}
\item Use the escape velocity definition $v_{\mathrm{esc}}=\sqrt{2GM/R}$ \hfill(1 point)
together with Hubble's law $v=H_0R$ \hfill(1 point)
and $M=\tfrac43\pi R^3\rho_c$. Substituting gives
\[\rho_c=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G}.\]
\hfill(4 points)
\item $H_0=2.2685\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. \hfill(2 points)
This gives $\rho_c=9.20\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. \hfill(2 points)
\item $M=\tfrac43\pi\rho_cR_\odot^3$. \hfill(1 point)
Then $N=M/M_{\mathrm{tennis}}$. \hfill(1 point)
Substitution gives $N=89.45$. Either 89 or 90 is acceptable. \hfill(3 points)
\end{enumerate}
