\begin{enumerate}
\item The mean synodic period of the Moon is $29.53059$ days. Dashain happened 12 synodic periods earlier. \hfill(1 point)
\[12\times29.53059=354.36708\,\mathrm{days}=354\,\mathrm{days}\ 8\,\mathrm{hours}\ 48\,\mathrm{minutes}.\]
\hfill(3 points)
Because 2024 is a leap year, there are 366 days between the two 12 Octobers. \hfill(1 point)
Dashain in 2023 will be 354 days before 12 October 2024. \hfill(3 points)
In other words, it was 12 days after 12 October 2023, which was 24 October 2023. \hfill(2 points)
\item Dashain in 2025 is 354 days 8 hours 48 minutes after 11 am on 12 October 2024. \hfill(1 point)
This is 11 days before 12 October 2025, at around 7:48 pm on 1 October 2025. \hfill(2 points)
Four days and 12 hours after this is 7:48 am on 6 October 2025. Therefore, Kojagrat Purnima falls on 6 October 2025. \hfill(2 points)
\end{enumerate}
