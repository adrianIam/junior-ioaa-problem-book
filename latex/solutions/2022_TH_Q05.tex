The angular resolution is
\[
\varphi_0=1.22\frac{\lambda}{D}
=1.22\frac{5\times10^{-7}}6=10.16\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{rad}.
\]
An angular displacement of $\varphi_0$ requires
\[
\tau=\frac{\varphi_0}{\omega}=\frac{\varphi_0}{2\pi}T.
\]
The generalized form of Kepler's third law for the Galaxy--Andromeda and
Sun--Earth systems is
\[
\left(\frac{T}{T_0}\right)^2
\frac{M_G+M_A}{M_0+M_P}=\left(\frac{R}{R_0}\right)^3.
\]
Since $M_P\ll M_0$,
\[
T^2=T_0^2\left(\frac{R}{R_0}\right)^3\frac{M_0}{M_G+M_A}.
\]
The supplied version concludes
\[
\tau=1.22\frac{\lambda}{2\pi D}T_0
\left(\frac{R}{R_0}\right)^{3/2}
\left(\frac{M_0}{M_G+M_A}\right)^{1/2}\approx10^3\,\mathrm{years}.
\]
