In the supplied solution, the required magnitude change is
\[
\Delta m=m_S-m_0=-26.84-(-12.7)=-14.14,
\qquad m=m_S=-26.84.
\]
For luminous intensity $I$, solid angle $\Omega$, flux $\phi$, and area $A$,
\[
I=\frac{\phi}{\Omega},\quad \Omega=\frac{A}{\Delta^2},\quad
E=\frac{\phi}{A}=\frac{I}{\Delta^2}=\frac{L}{4\pi\Delta^2}.
\]
\ioaafig{2022_TH_Q09-s1.pdf}
Pogson's relation gives
\[
\log\frac{E}{E_0}=-0.4(m-m_0),\qquad E_0=\frac{I}{r_0^2},\quad E=\frac{I}{r^2},
\]
\[
2\log\frac{r_0}{r}=-0.4\Delta m,\qquad
\log\frac{r_0}{r}=0.2\times14.14=2.828.
\]
Thus $10^{2.828}\approx675$ and
\[
r=\frac{r_0}{675}=\frac{384000\,\mathrm{km}}{675}\approx568.88\,\mathrm{km}.
\]
