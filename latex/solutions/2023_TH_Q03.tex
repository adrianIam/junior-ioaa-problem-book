The apparent magnitude of a star is defined as
\[
m=-2.5\log_{10}\left(\frac{F_x}{F_{x,0}}\right).
\]
\hfill\mbox{(4 points)}

Solve for the flux $F_x$ and add the fluxes corresponding to each star. This gives
\[
m_b=-2.5\log_{10}\left(10^{-0.4m_1}+10^{-0.4m_2}\right)=1.89\approx1.9\ \mathrm{mag}.
\]
