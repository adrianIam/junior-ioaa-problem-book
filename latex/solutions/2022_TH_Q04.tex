During the exposure interval, the satellite travels the arc
$AB=v\Delta t$. Equating gravity to the centripetal force gives
\[
K\frac{mM}{r^2}=\frac{mv^2}{r},\qquad
v=\sqrt{\frac{KM}{r}}=\sqrt{\frac{KM}{R+h}}.
\]
\ioaafig{2022_TH_Q04-s1.pdf}
The short arc can be treated as a line segment. The image forms in the focal
plane, and the similar triangles $ACS$ and $A'CS'$ give
\[
\frac{A'S'}{AS}=\frac{CS'}{CS},\qquad
\frac{l/2}{v\Delta t/2}=\frac{f}{h-f}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
l=\frac{f}{h-f}v\Delta t
=\frac{f}{h-f}\sqrt{\frac{KM}{R+h}}\,\Delta t.
\]
The answer is (a), with $K=G$.
