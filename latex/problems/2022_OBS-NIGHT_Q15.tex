Working time is 30 minutes, including five minutes to become familiar with
the tasks. The available equipment is a Newtonian telescope with EQ5 mount,
$200\,\mathrm{mm}$ primary mirror diameter and $1000\,\mathrm{mm}$ focal length,
plus $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ eyepieces.

\textbf{B. Observations using the astronomical telescope.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Point the telescope at $\beta$ Cyg and specify the star's type.
\item Point the telescope at Messier M45. State its constellation and object type.
\item Point the telescope at Jupiter with the $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ eyepiece.
  Draw what you observe and identify the Galilean satellites visible with
  the $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ eyepiece.
\end{enumerate}
