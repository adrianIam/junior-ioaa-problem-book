On the wall opposite to you, you will find signs named R, N and Y. Point the telescope towards each specific sign and, for each sign, answer the following.
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] Write the IAU name or Messier Catalogue Number, if applicable, of the celestial object found on the sign.
\item[(b)] Give the right ascension read from the telescope for the sign.
\item[(c)] Give the declination read from the telescope for the sign.
\end{description}
\textit{[The original object signs were supplied during the practical examination.]}
