You have 20 minutes in total. Avoid any direct viewing of the Sun. The telescope is already equipped
with a solar filter and the finder scope has been removed or covered. Write answers on the separate
answer sheet. Work on the question paper is not evaluated.
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] Carefully point the telescope towards the Sun and centre it in the field of view.
Ask the examiner to confirm successful targeting and record it on the answer sheet. If you cannot target
the Sun, the examiner will do so and all points for this question are lost. \hfill\mbox{[5 points]}
\item[(b)] Within the disk on the answer sheet, draw all sunspots visible through the telescope. \hfill\mbox{[10 points]}
\item[(c)] Measure the Sun's declination using the telescope. \hfill\mbox{[5 points]}
\item[(d)] Estimate the Sun's right ascension in hours and minutes. \hfill\mbox{[10 points]}
\item[(e)] What will be the Sun's approximate declination in 3 months? \hfill\mbox{[5 points]}
\item[(f)] Call the examiner and demonstrate how to ``scan'' the celestial equator with the telescope,
passing through all equatorial constellations. Give the examiner your answer sheet to record the result. \hfill\mbox{[10 points]}
\end{description}
