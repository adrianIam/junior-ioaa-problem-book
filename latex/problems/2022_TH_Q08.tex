In a future program, NASA plans to launch a spacecraft, aimed directly at
the Sun, without a human crew, to gather information, on its way to the Sun,
both about all the inner planets and, in particular, about the Sun.
They are known: the distance Earth--Sun, $r_{ES}=1.5\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{m}$;
the period of the Earth's rotation around the Sun, $T_E=3.15\times10^7\,\mathrm{s}$;
the radius of the Sun, $R_S\approx r_{ES}/200$.

If the launch of the spacecraft will be done in such a way that its motion
relative to the Sun is a free fall, then the approximate duration of the
Earth--Sun flight is
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] $t\approx3.6\times10^5\,\mathrm{s}$.
\item[(b)] $t\approx6.6\times10^6\,\mathrm{s}$.
\item[(c)] $t\approx5.6\times10^6\,\mathrm{s}$.
\item[(d)] $t\approx4.6\times10^6\,\mathrm{s}$.
\end{description}
