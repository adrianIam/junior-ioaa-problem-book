Working time is 30 minutes, including five minutes to become familiar with
the tasks. The available equipment is a Newtonian telescope with EQ5 mount,
$200\,\mathrm{mm}$ primary mirror diameter and $1000\,\mathrm{mm}$ focal length,
plus $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ eyepieces.

\textbf{A. Observations with the naked eye.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Estimate the sidereal time at the start of the test, within $\pm10$ minutes.
\item Find, within $\pm3^\circ$, the angular distances between
  $\alpha$ Per (Mirfak) and $\beta$ And (Mirach), and between $\beta$ Cas (Caph)
  and $\gamma$ Cyg (Sadar).
\item Specify two constellations at the meridian, their type of culmination,
  and their alpha stars.
\item Estimate Jupiter's right ascension within $\pm10$ minutes.
\item For $\beta$ Ari (Sheratan), estimate the apparent magnitude within
  $\pm0.4$, declination within $\pm3^\circ$, and right ascension within
  $\pm10$ minutes.
\end{enumerate}
