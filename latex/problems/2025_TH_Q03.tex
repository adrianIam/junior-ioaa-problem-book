Theodor is in a random city somewhere in the world. Using his knowledge of geography, he narrows down the possible locations to the four cities listed below. Then, on October 20th, he measures the time between sunrise and sunset and finds it to be $t=10^{\mathrm h}42^{\mathrm m}$.
Can you help him figure out which city he is in? Ignore atmospheric refraction and Sun's apparent size.
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] Compute the Sun's equatorial coordinates on that date. (Consider that the sun moves with constant velocity on the ecliptic from the autumn equinox onward.)
\item[(b)] Based on the measured daytime duration $t$, determine which of the four cities Theodor is in.
\item[(c)] On this day, exactly at the moment when the Sun sets for Theodor, determine the geographical coordinates of the point on Earth where the Sun is directly overhead.
\end{description}
