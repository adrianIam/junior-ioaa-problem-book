Two planets, A and B, orbit a star S in circular, coplanar orbits, in the same direction. The orbital radius
of planet A is 9 times smaller than the orbital radius of planet B. The orbital period of planet A is 10 Earth
days. An astronomer on Earth realizes that at 10:00 am on 26/9/2023, an observer on planet B would observe
a transit of planet A in front of star S. When is the next transit due to planet A to be seen by an observer
on planet B? All dates/times are given on Earth times. The masses of planets A and B are very small
compared to the mass of star S.
