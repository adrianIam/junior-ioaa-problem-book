Use the OM2--Chart and Time page below. The map is centered on the zenith and shows two constellations, C1 and C2, together with the horizon and a coordinate grid.
\begin{description}
\item[(OM2.1)] Identify the C1 and C2 constellations and write their Latin names or IAU abbreviations. \hfill[2]
\item[(OM2.2)] Draw an arrow each indicating the $\alpha$ stars of the C1 and C2 constellations. \hfill[2]
\item[(OM2.3)] One of the equinox points, either the vernal equinox (VE) or autumnal equinox (AE), is present on the map. Mark it with an X and label it VE or AE. \hfill[2]
\item[(OM2.4)] Identify which coordinate grid is present on the map. Mark one of ecliptic, azimuthal, galactic, or equatorial. \hfill[4]
\end{description}
