\textit{Telescope-round instructions.} There are five minutes to read the instructions before the round starts and 20 minutes with the telescope. Supervisors may assist in locking or unlocking the telescope axes on request. All telescopes have a 25\,mm eyepiece. The scales are calibrated so that the hour angle axis represents right ascension. Handle the telescope carefully.

On your telescope, the adjustment knobs are marked with 1 and 2. Mark with an X on your answer sheet the answers to the following questions.
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] Which coordinate does knob No.\ 1 refer to?
\item[(b)] Which coordinate does knob No.\ 2 refer to?
\end{description}
For each knob, choose altitude, right ascension, declination, or azimuth.
\textit{[The telescope and wall signs for this practical were supplied at the examination.]}
