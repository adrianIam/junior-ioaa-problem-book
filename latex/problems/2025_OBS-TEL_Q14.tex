On the wall opposite to your position, you will find a sign numbered 1. Point the telescope towards the specified sign and you will see a representation. For the star marked with the symbol $*$, answer the following, marking an X on the answer sheet.
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] What is the name of the coordinate marked with letter A?
\item[(b)] What is the name of the coordinate marked with letter B?
\item[(c)] What is the name of the coordinate marked with letter C?
\end{description}
Choose among azimuth, local hour angle, sidereal hour angle, zenith angle, altitude, declination, and meridian angle.
\textit{[The original wall sign was supplied during the practical examination.]}
