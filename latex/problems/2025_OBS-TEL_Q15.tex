On the wall opposite to your position, you will find a sign named O. Point the telescope towards the specified sign, where you should see a constellation. Answer the following.
\begin{description}
\item[(a)] Draw the constellation seen through the eyepiece on your answer sheet.
\item[(b)] Specify the IAU name or abbreviation of the constellation.
\item[(c)] Mark the Alpha star with a circle on your drawing.
\item[(d)] Write the IAU name of the Alpha star.
\item[(e)] Between stars Gamma ($\gamma$) and Beta ($\beta$) of this constellation, there is a Messier object. Specify its IAU name or Messier catalogue number.
\item[(f)] Specify the type of the object. Choose galaxy, supernova, star cluster, planetary nebula, dwarf planet, or reflection nebula.
\item[(g)] On your drawing, mark this Messier object's position with a triangle ($\Delta$).
\end{description}
\textit{[The original constellation sign was supplied during the practical examination.]}
