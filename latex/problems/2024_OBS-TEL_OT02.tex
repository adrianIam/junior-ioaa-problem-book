An animation of a planet and its moons will be displayed on the screen. The animation corresponds to a single location over a 3-day period.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Identify the planet shown in the images by ticking the correct option. Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, or Neptune.
\item Draw initial and final positions of the moons on your answer sheet and label the moons as A, B, C, and D. For example, if one moon is labeled as A in the first drawing, the same should be labeled as A in the second drawing.
\item Arrange the labels (A, B, C, D) of the observed moons according to their size from largest to smallest.
\end{enumerate}
