its circular orbit around the Sun; $v_0\approx7.9\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$,
the speed of a terrestrial satellite orbiting the Earth in a very low

\[\frac{M_{\mathrm{Earth}}}{R_{\mathrm{Earth}}}\ll
  \frac{M_{\mathrm{Sun}}}{R_{\mathrm{Earth-Sun}}}.\]

neglected during the motion of the body from the surface of the Earth to
the limit of the gravitational attraction of the Earth.

launched at a distance from the Sun equal to $1\,\mathrm{AU}$, so that it
leaves the Solar System forever, is
\begin{description}

\item[(b)] $v_B\approx32.32\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$.
