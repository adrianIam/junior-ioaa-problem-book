Starting from the law of conservation of energy, derive an expression for the total mechanical energy of a body of mass $m$ on an elliptical orbit, in terms of $G$, the mass of the object being orbited $M$, and the semi-major axis of the orbit $a$. Use your derivation to then find the ratio of the speeds at perihelion and aphelion, for an object that has an orbit with eccentricity $e$.
