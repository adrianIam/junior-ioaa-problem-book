\chapter*{About this edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this edition}
This collection brings together the individual Junior IOAA papers for
@@YEAR_RANGE@@. Problems are grouped by subject. Observational tasks form a
separate chapter, grouped by round. Each chapter includes its corresponding
official solutions.

Problem statements and solution text are transcribed as editable \LaTeX.
Figures, charts, and marked answer overlays are vector crops from the supplied
papers. The companion crop edition preserves the original page content.
Numerical values and mathematical notation should be checked against those
source papers when the books are used for teaching.

The original papers are the work of their authors and organising committees.
Editorial titles support topic navigation. Notes identify missing solutions
and differences between question and solution versions in the supplied archive.
